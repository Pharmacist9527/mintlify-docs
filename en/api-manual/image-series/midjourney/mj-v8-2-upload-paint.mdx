<Note>
  Midjourney has a built-in content moderation system. Each image is moderated individually: filtered images are left out of the results and the remaining images are delivered normally, so you may receive fewer images than usual. If at least one image passes, the task is `completed` and billed normally; if all images are filtered, the task ends as `failed` and the reserved credits are refunded in full. Please make sure your prompts and reference images comply with the content guidelines.
</Note>
