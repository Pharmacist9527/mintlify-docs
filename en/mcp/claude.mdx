Claude chat uses remote MCP connectors. CLI setup prompts are for [Claude Code](/en/mcp/claude-code), a terminal-capable coding agent.

## Add and sign in

1. Open [Claude connectors](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=EvoLink&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmcp.evolink.ai%2Fmcp), or add a custom connector under **Customize → Connectors**.
2. Name: `EvoLink`. URL: `https://mcp.evolink.ai/mcp`.
3. Review detected authentication settings, choose browser sign-in and Claude's recommended OAuth identity. No API key is needed.
4. Connect, sign in to EvoLink in the browser and approve.
5. Enable EvoLink under **+ → Connectors** in the chat and verify an actual `check_balance` response.

Team or enterprise workspaces may require a person with connector-management permission to add it before members connect individually. Plan access, labels and cross-device availability follow [Claude's official connector guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11175166-get-started-with-custom-connectors-using-remote-mcp) and your account's interface.

## Example

```text
Use EvoLink to find suitable models for a five-second landscape video.
Show parameters and a quote, and wait for my approval before submitting.
```

Queries are free. Tool permission, such as Allow once, does not approve arbitrary future paid tasks. See [billing and caps](/en/mcp/billing).

## References and rendering

Web/chat-desktop clients generally cannot execute a local upload command or reliably pass the original chat attachment to the model. Prefer public URLs; see [uploads](/en/mcp/files).

The client controls platform icons, tool cards and thumbnails. Always deliver original links and actual charges. Connection failures require checking workspace access, OAuth and network; see [troubleshooting](/en/mcp/troubleshooting). Do not regenerate to repair display issues.
