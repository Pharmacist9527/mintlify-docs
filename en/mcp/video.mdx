## Choose the input mode

| Task | Check |
|---|---|
| Text-to-video | Prompt, duration, aspect ratio, resolution and audio |
| Image-to-video | Reference count, first/last frame fields and image requirements |
| Reference video or background edits | Video-input support, clip length, resolution and audio requirements |
| Extension or upscaling | Specialized model and its supported `source_task_id` or media inputs |

Compare suitable Seedance or Wan models when none was specified. Respect explicit model choices. Use current `get_model` / `models show` parameters; do not add fields such as `video_urls` to models that do not support them.

## Example request

```text
Use EvoLink for a five-second landscape sailing clip: cream sails,
gray-blue sea, overcast soft light and documentary camera movement.
Check model, resolution and sound settings, then quote and wait for approval.
```

Quote the prepared input with MCP `estimate_cost` or CLI `estimate`. After approval, call `generate_video` or `evolink generate video --quote QUOTE_ID --confirm`.

## Reference-video costs

Provide references using the [upload guide](/en/mcp/files). Upload limits and model limits differ. An uploaded file may still exceed a model's limits on duration, size, resolution or audio.

<Warning>
Per-second video requests with `video_urls`, `video_url` or `source_task_id` may have reference costs missing from published prices. MCP marks these estimates as partial and refuses submission with `max_cost_usd`. Adding `media_seconds` does not resolve the gap. Do not remove the user's budget without permission.
</Warning>

Resolution, sound and references can affect pricing. See [billing and caps](/en/mcp/billing).

## Wait and deliver

Video is usually asynchronous. Retain the `task_id` and query it with `get_task`. CLI commands:

```bash
evolink tasks wait TASK_ID --timeout 1800 --json
evolink download TASK_ID --output ./sailing.mp4 --json
```

A wait timeout does not mean generation failed. Never resubmit to check progress. Deliver the original video link and charge. An inline first frame is only a preview, not the video itself. Clients may not embed video; always provide the original entry point. Soundtrack replacement, text overlays and local repairs require separate authorization.
