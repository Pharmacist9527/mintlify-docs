## Choose the task first

- **Music or songs:** inspect suitable models such as Suno for simple/custom modes, lyrics, style and instrumental output.
- **Speech or voiceover:** inspect speech models for text fields, language, voice and references. Do not substitute a music model.
- **Reference voice or multimodal inputs:** use only supported inputs and obey mutually exclusive reference rules.

Use `search_models` / `get_model`, or CLI `models search --type audio` and `models show`.

## Example requests

```text
Use EvoLink for a clear, natural voiceover saying “Welcome to EvoLink.
Start your next creation.” Find a suitable speech model, quote and wait for approval.
```

```text
Use EvoLink for a cheerful summer road-trip song. Check Suno's modes and
parameters, show the expected cost and output count, then wait for approval.
```

Models may use prompt, text or lyrics fields. One model's template does not apply to every audio model.

## Why not just add duration?

Some per-second audio models, including Seed Audio, do not let you set output duration. Adding `duration` to `input` just for a quote causes parameter validation to reject it.

`media_seconds` (CLI `--media-seconds`) is an estimate hint separate from model input. For generated audio of unknown length, it illustrates the cost **if** the output has that length. It is not a total or upper bound. Final billing uses measured generated duration.

You cannot establish `max_cost_usd` for this case by providing `media_seconds`. Explain the unknown duration and cost, preserve the user's budget, and ask whether they accept a task whose total cannot be known in advance. See [billing and caps](/en/mcp/billing).

## Submit, query and save

After approval, call `generate_audio` or use the saved audio quote:

```bash
evolink generate audio --quote QUOTE_ID --confirm --wait --json
evolink tasks get TASK_ID --json
evolink download TASK_ID --output ./audio.mp3 --index 1 --json
```

Match the output extension to the actual format; changing an extension does not convert media. Music tasks may return multiple tracks and cover images. Inspect `results`, deliver each relevant item and use its correct `--index` when downloading. Multiple outputs do not authorize further generation.

Report actual cost and original links when complete. Embedded playback depends on the client. Results usually expire after 24 hours; uploaded references are retained for approximately 72 hours.
