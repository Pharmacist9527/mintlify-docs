ChatGPT has no one-click add, so follow these steps on the web.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a custom MCP server">
    Open the [ChatGPT plugins page](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **+** and choose **Create custom MCP server**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the connection">
    Enter `EvoLink` as the name and `https://mcp.evolink.ai/mcp` as the **Server URL**; choose **OAuth** for authentication and keep the default registration method. Check **I understand and want to continue**, then click **Create as a plugin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install and sign in">
    On your [personal plugins page](https://chatgpt.com/plugins?view=personal), open EvoLink and click **+** to install it. When asked to sign in, click **Connect**, then sign in to EvoLink in your browser and click **Allow and connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a chat">
    Type `@` in the input and choose EvoLink.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can create it only in ChatGPT on the web, and you need a paid plan. In Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces, an admin must first grant the **Create custom MCP servers** permission. Whether your plan can use write actions such as generation depends on OpenAI's rules. ChatGPT's menus change often, and we haven't tested these steps ourselves yet.
</Note>

<Accordion title="ChatGPT desktop app">
  Open **Settings → MCP servers**, click **Add server**, choose **Streamable HTTP**, enter `https://mcp.evolink.ai/mcp`, save, then click **Authenticate** when sign-in is required. The approval page shows Codex as the app asking to connect; that's expected.
</Accordion>

## Try it

Send this in a chat; it only looks something up and costs nothing:

```text
Use EvoLink to check my account balance.
```

If the assistant calls an EvoLink tool and answers, you're connected. If EvoLink doesn't show up, type `@` in the input and check that EvoLink is listed. See [Check the connection](/en/mcp/tools#check-the-connection) and [Troubleshooting](/en/mcp/troubleshooting) for more.
