ChatGPT has no one-click add, so follow these steps on the web.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a custom MCP server">
    Open the [ChatGPT plugins page](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **Add** at the top right and choose **Create custom MCP server**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the connection">
    - **Icon** (optional): download the [EvoLink icon](https://cdn.evolink.ai/mcp/landing/evolink-plugin-icon-256.ccba2c71aa.png) (PNG, 256×256, 4 KB) and upload it here. Without it, the plugin has no icon.
    - Enter `EvoLink` as the **Name**; the **Description** is optional.
    - Under **Connection**, choose **Server URL** and enter `https://mcp.evolink.ai/mcp`.
    - Under **Authentication**, choose **OAuth** and leave **Advanced OAuth settings** as they are.
    - Check **I understand and want to continue**, then click **Create as a plugin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and sign in">
    Find EvoLink on your [personal plugins page](https://chatgpt.com/plugins?view=personal) and connect it when prompted. Your browser opens the EvoLink sign-in page; sign in and click **Allow and connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a chat">
    Type `@` in the input and choose EvoLink.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can create it only in ChatGPT on the web, and you need a paid plan. In Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces, an admin must first allow creating custom MCP servers. Whether your plan can use write actions such as generation depends on OpenAI's rules. We checked these steps against ChatGPT's screens in October 2026, but its menus change often, and we haven't tested the full sign-in and generation ourselves yet.
</Note>

<Accordion title="ChatGPT desktop app">
  We haven't checked where the desktop app adds servers. Look for the place to add a plugin or MCP server, enter the same URL and choose OAuth for authentication. When you sign in, the approval page shows Codex as the app asking to connect (Published by chatgpt.com); that's expected.
</Accordion>

## Try it

Send this in a chat; it only looks something up and costs nothing:

```text
Use EvoLink to check my account balance.
```

If the assistant calls an EvoLink tool and answers, you're connected. If EvoLink doesn't show up, type `@` in the input and check that EvoLink is listed. See [Check the connection](/en/mcp/tools#check-the-connection) and [Troubleshooting](/en/mcp/troubleshooting) for more.
