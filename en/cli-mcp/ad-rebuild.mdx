<span id="ad-rebuild" />
## What this workflow does

Give your assistant a short reference ad, your product and, if you have one, your creator. The assistant studies the reference, plans the same structure with your product, prepares the key images, generates the whole ad with sound in one video task, checks it against the plan, and hands you the result.

The assistant follows step-by-step rules that EvoLink sends it after sign-in, so you do not have to write a long prompt. You confirm one estimated total before anything is charged.

<span id="requirements" />
## Before you start

| You need | Details |
|---|---|
| An EvoLink sign-in | Connect through the [EvoLink plugin or remote MCP](/en/mcp/overview), or install and sign in to the [EvoLink CLI](/en/cli/setup). The workflow is offered to signed-in connections only; an API key alone does not open it |
| An assistant that can work with frames | An assistant that can run commands, for example Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, extracts frames on its own machine with ffmpeg and ffprobe. An assistant without a terminal, such as a chat in a browser, does this work with `make_frames`, a free tool that the remote MCP offers to signed-in connections |
| Enough balance | The estimate says whether your balance, and any EvoLink MCP spending limit you set, cover the run |

<Note>
An assistant that has neither a terminal nor the `make_frames` tool cannot do the frame work of this workflow. It tells you so before any charge.
</Note>

<span id="inputs" />
## What you provide

| Input | Details |
|---|---|
| Reference video | MP4 or MOV, **30 seconds or shorter**. If it is longer, the assistant asks which part to use |
| Product | One or more pictures of the product, what it does in one line, and the claims you can confirm. Claims in the reference are not carried over unless you confirm them for your product |
| Creator (optional) | Pictures of your creator. Without one, the assistant creates a fictional adult for the ad when a person is needed. It does not copy the person in the reference |
| Choices | Language, on-camera speech or voiceover, music, length (4 to 30 seconds), aspect ratio, and resolution: 480p, 720p, 1080p or 4K, or a 480p draft first. The assistant asks for what is missing in one message and offers defaults; if you give no resolution, it estimates 720p and lists the other choices with the total. Where your assistant can show choice buttons, it asks with them, and you can always type your own answer |

Use material that you own or are licensed to use. The workflow keeps the structure of the reference (hook, beats, actions, camera rhythm) and replaces the person, the product, the room, the wording and the claims.

<span id="cost" />
## One estimated total, confirmed once

Before you confirm, the assistant only does free work: it checks your sign-in and balance, reads the length and aspect ratio of the reference, counts its shots and works out the estimate. Then it shows you one total, for example:

```text
Estimated total: about $3.67 (249.4 credits) for a 10-second ad at 720p, with sound.
It covers analyzing the reference, the images for this ad, the video and a final check.
Other choices: 480p, 1080p or 4K, or a 480p draft that you can convert to 1080p or 4K later
(each conversion with its own price and confirmation). Pick one and I will quote that total instead.
Each step is charged as it runs. If the run stops before the ad is finished (you stop it, or a step keeps failing), the finished steps stay charged.
A failed image or video generation is not charged; I retry it at most twice, and you can continue from that step later without paying again for the finished steps.
To go ahead at 720p, reply "Confirm". If the work would cost more than this, I will ask you first.
```

- **The total is the budget.** After you confirm, the assistant does not ask about fees step by step. It comes back to you only if the work would go over the total.
- **Other choices are estimated for free.** If you pick another resolution or the draft, the assistant shows that total instead, and you still confirm only once.
- **What it covers:** analyzing the reference, the creator image when one is needed, the key images and an allowance for fixing some of them, one video with sound, and the final check.
- **What it does not cover:** converting a draft, which is priced and confirmed separately, and another whole video after one has been completed. If the finished ad does not meet the plan, the assistant explains what is off and what a new attempt costs, and waits for your decision.
- **If the balance is short,** the estimate says by how much and links to the top-up page. Nothing starts.
- Prices are your account's own prices at the time of the estimate; if those cannot be read, the estimate uses public default prices and says so. Charges follow actual usage. The estimate holds for 24 hours.

When the ad is delivered, the assistant tells you what was actually charged next to the estimate; ask it for a breakdown by task if you want the details. Every charge is listed in the [dashboard](https://evolink.ai/dashboard) history with its model and cost: images and the video as generation tasks, each video analysis as a Gemini model call. Sign in there with the same EvoLink account that your assistant uses.

<span id="steps" />
## What the assistant does

| Step | What happens | Charged |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Check and estimate | Length and aspect ratio of the reference, shot count, the estimated total. You confirm once | No |
| 1. Read the reference | Uploads the reference, analyzes its shots and its sound, and compares the result with frames | Yes |
| 2. Write the target | What changes and what stays, per shot: person, product, room, actions, speech, music | No |
| 3. Plan the whole ad | Timeline, the key images needed, and the single video task | No |
| 4. Build the key images | A creator reference when needed, then the first frame of every shot and the states in between. Each image is checked before the next step | Yes |
| 5. Build the video request | All key images and one complete description of picture and sound | No |
| 6. Generate and check | One video task for the whole ad with speech, music and sound effects. The result is analyzed against the target | Yes |
| 7. Deliver | The original video, a sheet of the key images, a short summary of the checks, the important open issues, and the cost: the estimate and the actual charge. A verdict per shot is available on request | No |

After you confirm, the assistant runs through to delivery. It stops only when the work would exceed the total, when a decision is yours alone (for example an action your product cannot do), when the finished ad would need a new attempt, or, if you chose a draft, when the draft is ready and you decide whether to convert it. If you want to see the key images before the video is made, say so when you confirm.

<span id="draft" />
## A draft first

With a draft, the whole ad is generated once at 480p and billed at the 480p price, so the estimated total covers the draft only. The assistant checks the draft like a finished ad and delivers it in the same way.

It then asks whether to convert the draft to 1080p or 4K, or to keep it as it is. Each conversion is a new task: the assistant shows its price and waits for your confirmation, and the conversion is charged on its own, outside the confirmed total. You can convert one draft more than once, for example to 1080p first and to 4K later; each conversion is charged.

A draft can be converted until its deadline. The assistant tells you the exact deadline, which a task read returns as `draft_expires_at`.

<span id="start" />
## Start a rebuild

1. Connect EvoLink in your assistant: install the [EvoLink plugin](/en/mcp/plugins), add the remote MCP ([Claude Code](/en/mcp/claude-code), [Codex](/en/mcp/codex), [Cursor](/en/mcp/cursor)), or set up the [EvoLink CLI](/en/cli/setup).
2. Put the reference video and your product pictures where the assistant can read them.
3. Ask in your own words, for example:

```text
Rebuild this ad with my product. Reference: ./reference.mp4. Product: ./product.png, a rose water face mist; it sprays a fine mist. English, 10 seconds, 720p, vertical.
```

The assistant fetches the workflow rules, does the free checks and comes back with the estimated total.

<span id="limits" />
## Limits and what to expect

- **Length:** the reference and the finished ad are at most 30 seconds.
- **One video task:** the whole ad is generated at once, with its sound. The workflow does not render shots separately, dub afterwards or edit the result.
- **Timing is approximate.** The video model treats the planned cut times as guidance. Cuts and actions can land about a second early or late; the delivery check reports where.
- **Text on screen and labels** are checked in the result and can still be distorted.
- **Content review:** if a model refuses the material, the assistant tells you and asks for different material. It does not reword a request to get past review.
- **Rate limits:** an account can start a limited number of video analyses per minute and per day. The message says how long to wait.
- **Playback:** 1080p and 4K videos are 10-bit HEVC files. Some players cannot open them; VLC and QuickTime can.

<span id="recovery" />
## If something is interrupted

Finished steps stay finished, and they stay charged. A later problem never requires reading the reference or generating accepted images again, and continuing does not charge them again.

| What happened | What the assistant does |
|---|---|
| The connection dropped during a video analysis | Sends exactly the same request again. The analysis that was already running is returned and is charged once |
| The service is busy | Waits as long as the message says and tries the same step again, at most two more times, then tells you |
| A generation task failed | That task was not charged; the steps finished before it stay charged. The assistant retries with the same inputs within the limit above. If it still fails, it tells you what has been charged so far, and you can continue from that step later |
| The balance or a spending limit is too low | Stops before the next charge, says what is finished and what is left to do, and continues after you top up or raise the limit |
| Uploaded files or result links expired | Uploads and result links are usually kept 7 days. The assistant uploads again from its local copies |

<span id="reference" />
## Tools and commands

Assistants use these on their own; you do not need to call them yourself.

| Remote MCP tool | CLI command | Purpose | Charged |
|---|---|---|---|
| get_media_workflow | `evolink workflow rules ad-rebuild` | Rules for the current step and the run ID | No |
| get_media_workflow with an estimate | `evolink workflow estimate ad-rebuild --seconds 10 --keyframes 5` | One estimated total for the planned run | No |
| analyze_video | `evolink video analyze FILE_URL_OR_TASK_ID --run RUN_ID --step STEP --purpose PURPOSE` | Analyze a video for a step of the workflow | Yes, by tokens |
| get_video_analysis | `evolink video analysis ANALYSIS_ID` | Collect an analysis that was still running | No |
| generate_workflow_media | `evolink estimate --model MODEL --input-file FILE --run RUN_ID --step STEP`, then `evolink generate image\|video\|audio --quote ID --confirm` | A paid generation of the run, noted with the run | Yes, at the model's price |
| record_workflow_delivery | `evolink workflow deliver TASK_ID --run RUN_ID` | Note which finished video was delivered | No |
| make_frames | Not in the CLI: the assistant uses ffmpeg on its own machine | Frames, crops, contact sheets and numbered image boards, for an assistant without a terminal | No |

Noting a generation with its run is a record that helps find it later; it changes neither the price nor the result. Uploads, estimates for single generations, generation and task queries use the usual [MCP tools](/en/mcp/tools) and [CLI commands](/en/cli/reference). See [models, costs, and quotas](/en/cli-mcp/models-and-billing) for how estimates and charges relate.
